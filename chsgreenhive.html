How GreenHive works

1. Each circle on the map is a zone. Its color is the temperature and the dark badge is how many times it was triggered in the last 24 h.
2. Tap a zone for its card, then mist it by hand, or use the orange + button for a guided request.
3. Auto mode starts misting when a zone passes your trigger temperature.
4. The line under each circle shows when it last triggered.
