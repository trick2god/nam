How CHS GreenHive works

1. The real map (OpenStreetMap or satellite) shows each zone colored by temperature, blue cool to red hot.
2. Tap a zone, then mist it by hand, or use the Trigger tab for a guided 3-step request.
3. Auto mode starts misting by itself when a zone passes your trigger temperature.
4. Every zone shows when it last triggered, and the Desk tab keeps the full log.
5. Readings here are simulated. To use real valves, replace go() with a call like:

POST /api/zones/court/mist
{"seconds":10,"mode":"auto"}
